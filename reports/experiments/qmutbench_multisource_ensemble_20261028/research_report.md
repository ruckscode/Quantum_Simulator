# QMutBench multi-source and ensemble research - 2026-10-28

## Result

No system is promoted. Existing binary production performance and model are unchanged. A new development-only analysis of saved three-class OOF predictions found a potentially complementary fixed probability blend, but it was measured on the same 12 origins used for candidate inspection and is not independent confirmation.

The task in this new analysis is QMutBench three-class research: Survivor=0, WOO=1, OPO=2. It must remain separate from the binary production task (WOO/OPO=detected, Survivor=undetected), whose established five-fold development champion remains mutation-aware Extra Trees at macro F1 0.7933 on 578,117 rows and 305 origins. Those binary and three-class metrics are not comparable.

## Data, source contribution, and transfer

The source inventory is in `source_inventory.csv`. The three-class experiment used 205 records from 12 development origins, balanced by design across classes (72/68/65), with identical IDs, labels, and three grouped folds for all six classical/QNN candidates. The artifacts were generated by the existing 12-origin exploratory campaign and its two extensions. The 36-origin reserved development confirmation described by the QNN campaign was not used here.

QMutBench remains the only locally validated source for this specific mutation-outcome target. Bugs4Q archives and extracted files are present, but the saved validator at `reports/benchmarks/bugs4q_validation_report.json` found zero cases at its configured root. The earlier provenance audit records zero eligible independent recipe roots and unavailable independent behavioral labels. Bugs4Q bug/fix labels also do not map to WOO/OPO/Survivor. QBugs, Qolumbina, and the ecosystem defect-report source have no locally verified common-label row set in this campaign. Therefore source-specific supervised training, cross-source pretraining, source contribution combinations, and multitask heads could not be evaluated validly. No false label mapping or synthetic row was introduced.

## Model diversity and ensemble results

All probabilities below are the saved fold-specific OOF probabilities from the matched three-class development pilot. Macro F1 is primary.

| Candidate | Strategy | Macro F1 | Accuracy | Balanced accuracy |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Extra Trees 4 features | classical | 0.4669 | 0.4927 | 0.4846 |
| Logistic Regression 4 features | classical | 0.4407 | 0.4634 | 0.4572 |
| RBF SVC 4 features | classical | 0.4306 | 0.4390 | 0.4348 |
| Angle VQC | quantum | 0.2998 | 0.3220 | 0.3180 |
| Amplitude VQC | quantum | 0.3086 | 0.3366 | 0.3445 |
| Angle VQC with trainable linear readout | hybrid | 0.3218 | 0.3561 | 0.3649 |
| Equal mean of all six probability vectors | fixed blend | **0.4919** | **0.5073** | **0.5007** |
| Logistic stacker, outer fold held out | fitted stacking | 0.4212 | 0.4341 | 0.4284 |

The all-six blend improves the in-sample-to-this-analysis pooled OOF score by 2.50 percentage points over the best individual result. Its origin-bootstrap 95% interval is 0.4241–0.5571; the best individual Extra Trees interval is 0.4042–0.5264. These broad, overlapping intervals are descriptive only: blend choice was evaluated on these same 12 origins. The fold-held-out stacker was fitted on the other two folds' base OOF probabilities per outer fold, but with just 12 origins its meta-training set was small and it underperformed. Its score is not a reason to tune C or search more stackers on these origins.

Pairwise disagreement and correct/wrong overlap are saved in `pairwise_error_complementarity.csv`; per-origin results and per-class metrics are saved separately. The QNNs did make some unique correct predictions, but their weak standalone performance and the small slice do not establish that a QNN is a useful member of a larger production ensemble. The equal classical probability mean scored 0.4774 macro F1 (see `candidate_metrics.json`); the all-model blend is higher on this slice, but the QNN contribution is not established without an untouched-origin confirmation. Conditional routing was not fitted: no inference-time rule was predeclared and 12 origins are inadequate for a credible nested router.

Probability averaging, probability-fitted outer-fold stacking, pairwise errors, and per-origin performance were tested. No search over weights, thresholds, routing conditions, or feature-selection rules was made. Runtime for the saved-prediction analysis and 2,000-origin-bootstrap summaries was about 24 seconds. The underlying candidate runtime and simulator costs are documented in the original QNN campaign report. A new model fit and memory claim were not made here; only stored OOF probability files were read.

## Decision and next evidence needed

The fixed blend is not promoted because it was assessed on already inspected exploratory origins. It should be treated as a predeclared candidate for the 36 reserved development origins under their existing protocol, together with the strongest matched classical model, if that confirmation is authorized by the original plan and is run once. Do not re-use its outcomes for repeated ensemble tuning. Until then, the production model remains unchanged and the best validated binary development result remains the existing Extra Trees champion.

No frozen-test rows, labels, or metrics were read for this campaign. The historical frozen-test evaluator was not run. The original three-class campaign notes a prior caveat: an earlier version parsed raw target columns in chunks before filtering to development IDs, although no frozen labels were retained or used in selection. This audit reuses the saved development-only OOF predictions and makes no stronger claim about that historical data-access path.

## Reproduction

Run `python experiments/qmutbench_multisource_ensemble_audit.py` from the repository root with the campaign's scikit-learn, NumPy, and pandas environment. The script reads only the three saved development OOF prediction files under `reports/experiments/qmutbench_three_class_qnn_campaign_20261027/` and writes its outputs in this directory. It does not load raw QMutBench rows or the frozen-test evaluator.
