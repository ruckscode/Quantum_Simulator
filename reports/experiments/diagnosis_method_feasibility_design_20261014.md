# Diagnosis method feasibility and validation design (2026-10-14)

## Disposition

The held-out acceptance failure remains a frozen failure. The seven outcomes and their root-cause work are recorded in `heldout_diagnosis_root_cause_20261014.json`; this document does not replay or reinterpret those cases. Existing finite-shot no-go artifacts remain authoritative. No thresholds, labels, held-out criteria, or simulator behavior were changed to improve those outcomes.

The current observations support three distinct claims with different limits:

1. **Output-distribution anomaly detection is conditionally testable.** With a declared reference distribution and independent observed counts, a calibrated goodness-of-fit or two-sample procedure can test a specified null. Its type-I error depends on the sampling model, support handling, shot allocation, and any data-dependent choices. The current uncalibrated TVD guard does not establish that property and prior registered controls did not meet the acceptance contract. A practical effect-size statement also needs an uncertainty interval and a predeclared meaningful margin; a p-value alone cannot establish useful magnitude.
2. **Cause attribution is not identifiable from output distributions in general.** If two distinct causes induce the same distribution for every outcome observable in the experiment, their likelihoods are identical and no number of additional shots can distinguish them. The existing identifiability analysis establishes this limit. Attribution requires additional independent observables, such as a source diff for program changes or calibrated hardware telemetry for physical faults. A source diff supports a syntactic candidate, not semantic proof; simulator-injected noise is not independent evidence of a real device fault.
3. **Localization is evidence-source-specific.** Comparing supplied expected and submitted operation sequences can produce operation-level candidates. This does not prove that a candidate caused the measured result, especially for commuting or observationally equivalent operations. A simulator model can validate software behavior but cannot establish physical hardware localization.

Prior work also treats diagnosis as an explicit circuit-fault model and evaluates against simulator/device observations (for example, [Li et al., IEEE VTS 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/VTS56346.2023.10140030)). That establishes relevant prior art, not novelty or validity of this repository's method. Novelty and protocol overlap need independent review before any publication claim.

## Candidate validation design (proposal only; no study executed)

Separate the objectives and endpoints. Do not combine them into a single diagnosis-accuracy score:

| Objective | Unit and independent truth | Candidate endpoint | Evidence limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Detect output change | Circuit family is the split/cluster unit; a blinded, preregistered mutation or controlled intervention defines truth independently of model inputs | Type-I rate on exact/equivalent controls, sensitivity by predeclared effect-size strata, and interval coverage | Applies only to declared circuits, noise, shot budgets, and distributional hypotheses |
| Attribute a program change | Independently adjudicated source transformation and affected operation, hidden during scoring | Category/location accuracy plus abstention coverage and selective risk | Source-derived labels cannot also appear as model features or be used to tune cutoffs |
| Attribute hardware cause | External device/job identifiers, calibration and telemetry, independent of simulated injection labels | Cause-specific sensitivity/false attribution, with abstention | Simulator-generated hardware labels do not count as hardware evidence |

Before collecting study data, freeze: the sampling frame and family-disjoint split; the exact reference and observation sampling design; null/equivalence controls; a practically meaningful distributional effect margin; the type-I error and joint sensitivity/coverage requirements; multiplicity handling; an abstention policy; independent label adjudication; missing/unsupported-case rules; analysis code and randomization; and compute, storage, runtime, and shot budgets. Use a sealed family-level holdout that cannot be used to choose statistics, bins, margins, calibration, or thresholds. Ground truth must be unavailable to feature construction and analyst tuning.

Power planning must reproduce the full planned analysis, including clustered families, class/effect strata, abstentions, multiplicity, and confidence intervals. Use analytically justified bounds where their assumptions apply and verify operating characteristics with simulations generated independently of the final holdout. Report the family count and effective independent sample size, not just circuits or shots. Define minimum resources and a stop rule before any execution. A pilot may estimate nuisance parameters only on separate development families and may not silently become the acceptance study.

## Go/no-go decision

**No-go for an expensive study now.** Existing method and candidate-selection records show unresolved calibration/control and power/design deficiencies; independent benchmark-compatible truth and a defensible family sampling frame are absent. The three prerequisites for a new proposal are (a) a method that passes development-only null/effect simulations under the frozen acceptance contract, (b) a complete preregistered protocol and family-level power/resource analysis reviewed independently, and (c) sufficient independent ground truth. Only then can a separately authorized bounded pilot be considered. The existing frozen acceptance remains unchanged and failed. This proposal is not evidence that any target accuracy is achievable.



## Independent-review readiness specification (prepared, not executed)

### Sampling frame and family assignment

The existing memo requires a family-level frame but does not define an instantiated population or a reproducible selection list. Before a confirmatory protocol can be reviewed, the frame must be a versioned, finite manifest of all eligible circuit families in a declared target domain: name the circuit source/release or generation algorithm and commit, supported gate/measurement subset, qubit/depth ranges, and date/eligibility rules. Each frame row must include a stable family root, canonical circuit/source hashes, structural stratum, allowed fault/control conditions, and an inclusion/exclusion disposition with reason. A circuit plus mutations, parameter variants, reordered equivalents, and repeated seeds derived from a common template stay in one family cluster. Family IDs must be audited against source lineage so near-duplicates cannot cross folds under separate IDs.

Select whole families before collecting diagnosis outcomes. If stratifying, define strata from pre-outcome circuit structure and independently assigned intervention class; record the family-level sampling probabilities and nonresponse/unsupported disposition. Keep development, protocol selection, calibration, and sealed confirmatory sets disjoint by family root and source lineage. Publish a hash of the sealed frame and split before labels/outcomes are joined. These definitions specify what is needed; the repository currently has no independent target-population manifest, sampling probabilities, or independent adjudicated labels, so no valid sample size or external sampling claim follows yet.

### Independent truth and competing explanations

For software program-change truth, an adjudicator who did not author/tune the detector should verify the intended-versus-submitted transformation and affected operation from immutable source/circuit artifacts, blind to diagnosis output. Source diff alone labels a syntactic change, not that it caused an output anomaly. Exact/equivalent controls need independent semantic verification on development material. Hardware-cause truth must come from the physical job identity, compiled layout, calibration/telemetry and an independent adjudicator; a simulator-injected hardware profile is only a constructed scenario label. Detection truth concerns a predeclared distributional null/effect and must be decided independently from method scores.

At minimum, the protocol must keep these alternatives distinct: finite-shot fluctuation under no change; a real distribution change from a program mutation; a hardware or readout effect; observationally equivalent program/hardware causes; compiler routing/layout or classical-bit interpretation error; stale/missing calibration or drift; malformed/unsupported execution; duplicated family lineage; and outcome-informed binning, threshold, or feature selection. Output counts alone cannot resolve the observationally equivalent-cause case.

### Endpoints, power, and resources

The currently frozen diagnosis criteria remain unchanged and failed. Any future study must report separate detection, cause-attribution, localization, abstention/selective-risk, unsupported, and execution-failure outcomes; it cannot replace the frozen result. Detection null/effect margins, family-level type-I error, strata, interval procedure, multiplicity, minimum useful coverage and stopping/decision rules must be frozen using development families before opening a sealed family holdout. The existing `finite_shot_joint_power_analysis.md` is conditional: it assumes independent sampled families and illustrative true rates, notes that ICC/joint endpoint dependence are unknown, and does not establish the actual candidate's power. Its 900-family example (48.384 million shot executions; modeled 199.5-minute runtime) is not authorization or a sufficient design claim. Actual nuisance rates, target frame, ICC, cost envelope, and coverage-stratum definition remain missing.

Independent review materials can include the present protocol, identifiability analysis, power calculations, source/data schema, analysis code hash, and explicit no-go conditions. Reviewers must be independent of method implementation and must approve the target population, label adjudication, family independence, power assumptions, and resource/cost cap. The current novelty review is not complete; no novelty or causal-validity claim is made.

## Research milestone addendum: independent evaluation contract (2026-10-15)

### Research question and target claim

The target claim is conditional and deliberately narrower than “fault diagnosis works”: **for circuits in a future, named population and supported execution domain, can an evidence-graded method detect a prespecified output-distribution change, and can it make a program/hardware cause and target claim only when an independent evidence source supports that claim?** The study would estimate separate detection, ambiguous-versus-single-cause disposition, exact single-cause attribution, source-localization, hardware-localization, and abstention outcomes. It would not produce a single combined accuracy score.

The population is not yet instantiated. A protocol reviewer must choose whether the first target is (a) source-level program-fault triage in supported simulator/executor circuits or (b) diagnosis on a named hardware backend. Those are separate populations and studies. A simulator intervention may support development of software logic, but cannot count as an independently established hardware cause. The present work proposes a protocol; it does not instantiate a target frame or establish that the candidate method succeeds.

### Candidate method, not implemented as a detector

The candidate is a two-layer **distribution screen plus evidence-constrained disposition**. Its assumptions are independent, correctly recorded count samples under a predeclared reference/measurement protocol; compatible outcome encodings; and an explicit account of execution blocks or family-level dependence. If these assumptions are not supported by the sampling design, the screen returns “not evaluable” rather than a calibrated result.

1. **Distribution screen.** Preserve integer counts and sample sizes. When both reference and submitted outcomes are independently sampled under exchangeable conditions, use a prespecified count-based discrepancy statistic (TVD is an interpretable candidate) and calibrate its null by a two-sample permutation distribution that preserves group sizes. Exact finite-sample level for the equality-of-distributions null relies on independent samples and exchangeability; it does not automatically cover unequal nuisance processes or clustered/drifting execution ([Chung & Romano, *Annals of Statistics*, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1214/13-AOS1090)). Specify the number/seed of Monte Carlo permutations before analysis and use a finite-simulation adjustment; use exhaustive permutations where practical. If the reference probabilities are fixed independently rather than sampled, use a one-sample multinomial goodness-of-fit procedure with a null simulation that accounts for estimated parameters; sparse multinomial calibration needs its own justification ([Simonoff, *JASA*, 1986](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1986.10478365)). Do not mix these designs or treat normalized frequencies as if their shot uncertainty were absent. A test result is evidence about distributional change only; pair it with a development-specified effect-size interval. The existing fixed `max(0.05, 1/sqrt(shots))` TVD rule is not this calibrated procedure and remains unchanged for the frozen acceptance test.
2. **Evidence-constrained cause disposition.** Reuse the existing reference/submitted operation comparison and program-localization candidates as *source evidence*. Treat hardware-localizer scores as *candidate evidence* only when accompanied by separately captured, versioned calibration/telemetry and job/layout provenance. Neither candidate list nor a distribution p-value is a cause label. Report a single cause only if the preregistered independent evidence rule selects one cause; retain a set-valued “ambiguous/equivalent” disposition when multiple causes remain compatible; return unresolved/abstain for missing, conflicting, unsupported, or non-identifying evidence. A syntactic source difference with no behavioral signal is reported as a source mismatch plus indeterminate behavioral cause, not as a detected fault. A distribution change without source or physical evidence is an anomaly, not a causal attribution.
3. **Shot and abstention policy.** Fix shot strata and stopping rules before the test set is opened. Do not add adaptive stopping or tune a threshold from the sealed families. Develop any operating rule on development families, set its calibration on disjoint calibration families, then hash/freeze it. Report abstention and coverage explicitly; an abstention on an independently adjudicated ambiguous/equivalent case is a meaningful outcome, while missing/conflicting truth is a data-quality disposition and cannot be silently scored as a correct abstention.

This proposal reuses `diagnose_circuit(reference_circuit=...)`, `compare_counts`/the current TVD statistic, operation-level candidates from `localize_program_fault`, and hardware candidates from `localize_hardware_fault`. The existing engine samples both programs and routes thresholded TVD plus source/model evidence into one category; the current statistic does not provide a calibrated finite-shot p-value, and the localizer scores are not calibrated causal probabilities. No production detector or simulator code was changed. The candidate's statistical validity remains hypothetical until its sampling assumptions and null calibration are independently reviewed and demonstrated on development material.

### Case schema and independent truth

The machine-checkable contract is implemented in `experiments/diagnosis_evaluation_protocol.py`. A case record keeps these fields separate:

- **Split/evaluation envelope:** opaque `case_id`, `family_root_id`, `lineage_root_id`, preassigned `partition` (`train`, `calibration`, or `test`), and canonical `family_fingerprint_sha256`. The identifiers and partition are evaluator metadata, never predictor features.
- **Observable input:** reference and submitted operation sequences; integer reference and submitted outcome counts; their recorded shot totals; measurement schema; and, when this endpoint is in scope, a telemetry slot containing only the telemetry actually available to the method. Raw source differences can be candidate evidence. Mutation names/IDs, true cause/target, adjudication outcome, internal simulator state, generated labels and prior diagnostic output are forbidden in this payload.
- **Provenance:** hashes for source, observation artifact and protocol, plus Python implementation/version and dependency/runtime versions. A real-hardware case would additionally need immutable job ID, backend/calibration version, compiled layout, and raw-result provenance in the externally reviewed schema.
- **Adjudicator-only envelope:** review status, distribution truth, cause disposition and possible-cause set, source/hardware target disposition and target sets, reviewer identifiers and evidence references. Statuses explicitly represent `SINGLE_CAUSE`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `OBSERVATIONALLY_EQUIVALENT`, `CONFLICTING`, `UNRESOLVED`, `MISSING`, and `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`. A single-cause label must have exactly one possible cause; ambiguous/equivalent labels must preserve at least two. The predictor never receives this envelope.

Program-fault truth should be grounded in immutable intended/submitted source artifacts and independently reviewed transformation/target evidence. That establishes what source change was made; behavioral causality additionally needs a controlled, appropriately paired execution design. Hardware-cause truth requires physical job identity, compiled layout, independent calibration/telemetry and blinded adjudication; injected simulator settings are synthetic development labels only. Observational equivalence must be established from semantics or a reviewed intervention model, not inferred merely because a finite-sample test did not reject equality. Missing and conflicting cases remain in the manifest and are accounted for under a preregistered endpoint-specific missingness rule.

The validator rejects feature/label and identity leakage, malformed count/sample-size pairs, duplicate case IDs, exact duplicate family fingerprints under different family roots, and any family or lineage crossing train/calibration/test. Its registered endpoints separately track general cause disposition and exact single-cause attribution: an ambiguous/equivalent case is eligible for the disposition/abstention strata, never silently counted as a known single-cause success; if a single-cause-only endpoint requires that case, the contract fails closed. It returns confirmatory prerequisites as blocked when the target/use, hashed frame/split/preregistration/acceptance/power/resource records, independent review, or endpoint-specific test truth is incomplete. “Prerequisites recorded” means only that the documented contract is structurally complete; it does **not** certify truth quality, family independence, adequacy of sample size, power, method performance, or external validity. Approximate near-duplicate families still require a prespecified similarity audit and independent resolution; hash equality only catches exact duplicate family fingerprints.

### Sampling, partitions, endpoints, and analysis

Before any confirmatory outcomes are collected, an independent custodian must publish a finite, versioned frame of eligible families for the selected target domain, including source/release or generation algorithm, supported instruction/measurement scope, eligibility/exclusion dispositions, canonical source/circuit hashes and audited family/lineage roots. Select whole families before outcome generation. Keep all templates, parameter variants, mutants, repeated seeds and recipe descendants under the same root. Assign disjoint roots to development (candidate/statistic selection), calibration (operating policy only), and sealed test (one final evaluation); create the split manifest before labels are joined. A new seed or mutation of a familiar template is not an independent test family. No existing 45-case held-out family may be used for selection or tuning.

Report distinct endpoints:

- **Detection:** false-alarm rate on independently established no-change controls and sensitivity by independently specified change/effect strata; accompany rates with effect-size intervals and calibration checks. The precise null, practical margin and error allocation need domain review.
- **Cause disposition:** class-wise confusion including ambiguous/equivalent and abstain categories; exact cause-set agreement where truth is set-valued; coverage and selective risk on independently adjudicated cases. Do not score ambiguous truth as an arbitrary single class.
- **Localization:** operation/qubit target-set exact and top-k recovery for source faults; component target-set recovery for hardware cases. Report each only on cases with independently supported target truth, and keep source and hardware denominators separate.
- **Abstention:** coverage, wrong-confident-cause rate, and correct abstention on independently adjudicated ambiguous/equivalent cases. Report missing/conflicting/unresolved truth separately; do not count these as successful abstentions.

Keep every family as the uncertainty unit. Use family-cluster bootstrap or a justified family-level model, with paired resampling for method comparisons; do not use shots, repeated seeds, or within-family variants as independent families. Report class-wise intervals, family count, family-size distribution, and the number of families contributing to each endpoint. Select one primary endpoint for the intended use before preregistration; adjust or clearly label secondary/multiple comparisons. Declare exclusions, nonresponse, unsupported cases, missing-data handling and all analyses before outcomes are unsealed.

No numeric acceptance floor is proposed here. The intended-use owner and independent reviewers must set the tolerated false-alarm and missed-fault risks, minimum useful coverage, localization utility/cost, and any noninferiority/superiority margins before calibration is frozen. If those decisions cannot be justified for a named use, the work is descriptive feasibility estimation and cannot pass a confirmatory release gate. The frozen 7/45 acceptance and prior 30/60 finite-shot result remain historical failed/no-go evidence and are not relaxed by this proposal.

### Power, resources, and authorization gate

A defensible power analysis is presently impossible. Required inputs that are absent include the named target frame and prevalence, the family-level variance/ICC and within-family endpoint correlations, plausible development-only effect sizes and control rates, ambiguity/unsupported/missing proportions, the selected primary endpoint and multiplicity plan, and the intended-use acceptance margins. The existing power documents contain conditional arithmetic examples (including a 900-family/48.384-million-shot scenario); they are not a justified sample size, measured runtime, or authorization. Do not reuse their illustrative rates as facts.

After the frame and endpoints are independently selected, power should simulate the complete family-cluster sampling and decision procedure under development-only nuisance assumptions. Vary prevalence, family dependence, ambiguity, missingness, shot allocation, and paired detector discordance in sensitivity analyses. Choose family counts only from this review; if the needed frame is unavailable, explicitly narrow the intended claim or stop. Resource estimates must separately account for families × conditions × (reference shots + submitted shots), permutation/resampling compute, memory, storage, runtime, backend limits and recovery. Reuse saved counts across methods only when the preregistered design permits it. A bounded local timing check can estimate software cost after the protocol exists; it cannot predict paid quantum service cost or justify a large run.

Before an experiment is considered for authorization, require all of: a named target population/use; a versioned family frame with source/lineage and duplicate audit; independent blinded adjudication rules and assigned reviewers; development evidence supporting the detector's assumptions; family-disjoint hashed partitions; frozen endpoint/acceptance/missing-data and multiplicity decisions; a family-level power report with justified nuisance assumptions; a reviewed resource, stop and recovery plan; passing schema/provenance/feature-separation checks; independent written protocol approval; and separate explicit execution authorization. None of these conditions authorizes a paid or hardware run by itself.

## Development-only method advancement (2026-10-15)

### Evidence selection and limits

The finite-shot reports identify healthy-control false alarms as the most actionable technical limitation for a development candidate. In the existing saved 120-record-per-cell comparison, the fixed TVD rule flagged all identical and measurement-equivalent controls at 256 shots, and 104/120 to 110/120 at 1,024 shots. The pooled-label permutation comparator flagged 2/120 to 8/120 controls in those reported low-shot cells. This supports a narrowly stated hypothesis: a count-based null test may control false alarms better than the current scalar `1/sqrt(shots)` TVD guard under the recorded simulator sampling design. It does not establish validity outside those conditions.

Those saved outputs are historical and were not regenerated or retuned in this phase. Their design uses one four-qubit paired-CNOT recipe with repeated templates/conditions, not independent circuit families; its untouched-validation results have already been examined and are sealed no longer. They are therefore usable as bounded, synthetic calibration evidence for motivating the candidate, not as fresh validation or population-level evidence. The three-family reference-generalization fixture is likewise a synthetic engineering fixture and is not used for candidate selection. The frozen 45-case acceptance evidence remains entirely excluded.

### Isolated candidate

`experiments/diagnosis_candidate_methods.py` now contains a development-only, seeded two-sample permutation test over integer outcome counts. It returns a Monte Carlo p-value using pooled-label permutations and the add-one correction. Its null interpretation requires independent, exchangeable shot outcomes from the two executions. A p-value is not a probability that a fault exists. The helper is not connected to the simulator's default detector and makes no cause attribution, source/hardware localization, or abstention decision. It has only contract tests on tiny synthetic vectors; these verify deterministic behavior and input rejection, not statistical operating characteristics.

The saved calibration comparison also identifies the main trade-off: at 256 shots the permutation detector found only 14/120 subtle-effect records; at 1,024 shots it found 36/120. Thus lowering false alarms can miss subtle changes at low shot counts. No threshold or criterion is adjusted here. The current 30/60 registered finite-shot result remains a no-go, and no finite-shot campaign was run in this phase.

### Next development experiment, not authorized

Before any new performance evaluation, prepare a new, versioned development-only set of independently generated circuit families with paired reference/submitted sampling, known simulator intervention labels, at least one no-change control stratum, measurement-equivalent controls, and predeclared subtle/moderate/large effects. Freeze a family-disjoint calibration split and use it only to select a candidate test and Monte Carlo resolution. Use a newly generated untouched development-validation family split to estimate false-alarm and sensitivity intervals, stratified by shots and effect size; preserve detection separately from cause disposition, localization, and abstention. Include a simulator family/lineage duplicate audit and a record-level count/provenance audit before execution. The existing validation split cannot serve this role because its outputs were already inspected.

This design can establish simulator-only behavior over the sampled recipe frame. It cannot establish physical hardware causes, broad circuit-family generalization, or independent truth quality. A confirmatory study still requires a named target/use, independent frame and adjudication, reviewed acceptance/multiplicity rules, family-level power/resource analysis, independent review, and separate authorization. Do not generate or execute this new set until its bounded size and resource plan are justified; do not use the frozen 45 cases.
