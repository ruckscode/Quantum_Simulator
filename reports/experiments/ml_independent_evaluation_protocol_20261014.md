# Independent ML evaluation protocol (review draft, 2026-10-15)

## Status and scope

This is an executable protocol specification for independent review, not a study result or authorization to run one. The preserved current pilot at `ml_data_foundation_pilot_20261014_final/` validates serialization, provenance, predictor/label separation, lineage grouping and Git checkout identity only. It contains nine simulator-generated rows from three recipe roots, has zero independent adjudications, and is explicitly unfit for training, calibration, accuracy scoring or acceptance decisions. The earlier dated pilot directories are preserved development checkpoints; earlier drafts predate the internal-run manifest or Git checkout identity. They are superseded by the `20261014_final` pilot for current verification. No values from any pilot are evidence about ML performance.

## Target population and sampling frame

The target population must be declared for one use before sampling: (A) supported software circuit families in named, versioned source releases, for offline program-change triage; or (B) physical circuit executions on named backend families and time/calibration windows, for hardware anomaly triage. These populations are separate protocols and cannot share a pooled accuracy claim. A frozen frame must enumerate eligible independent family roots, source release/commit, canonical reference-circuit hash, supported operations, width/depth/measurement strata, eligibility date/rules, intervention classes, inclusion probability, exclusions and reason. Include the full eligible frame and its SHA-256 in the preregistration. Sample whole roots before executing or joining outcomes. A lineage audit must join mutations, parameter variants, templates, reordered/equivalent circuits, repetitions and recipe descendants; related items share one root. Near-duplicate screening is an alert for blinded adjudication, not a post hoc numeric independence threshold. If the proposed frame is unavailable or cannot be independently enumerated, the evaluation does not start.

## Independent labels and adjudication

Keep three distinct targets and truth sources:

1. **Distribution-change detection:** exact/no-change or intervention truth from a preregistered intervention on a held-out family, with an independent record of the intervention and observation acquisition. Simulator-generated counts are development fixtures only.
2. **Program-cause attribution/localization:** two independent reviewers, blinded to model output and features, inspect immutable expected/submitted source or circuit artifacts. They adjudicate whether a syntactic change exists, whether its semantic behavior changes the measured distribution under the declared circuit, and a location when supportable. Source diff alone is not causal truth. Disagreement, equivalent mutations and insufficient evidence are `AMBIGUOUS`/`UNRESOLVED`; never coerce them to a single class.
3. **Hardware-cause attribution:** physical backend/job identifier, compiled circuit/layout, contemporaneous calibration/telemetry and independent review are required. A simulator-injected profile is never hardware ground truth.

Store label records outside predictor artifacts with provenance, reviewer role, blind status, evidence hashes, adjudication status and rationale. Labels may be `NORMAL`, `PROGRAM_FAULT`, `HARDWARE_ANOMALY`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `FAILED` or `UNLABELED` according to the target and predeclared ontology. Missing/ambiguous labels remain excluded from primary supervised scoring and appear in a disposition table. Report an agreement statistic and disagreement counts before consensus; reviewers cannot tune the model or criteria.

## Predictors, partitions and leakage controls

Freeze the predictor schema and extraction code before collection. The five candidate observable summaries are TVD, JS divergence, Hellinger distance, observed entropy and maximum observed probability; they must be recomputed only from declared observation/reference inputs. Calibration values are candidate only if independently measured before outcomes and available in the intended deployment; configured simulator profile fields are excluded. Exclude labels, diagnosis outputs, fault descriptions/IDs, operation-index/qubit labels, configuration-derived hardware parameters, validation labels, run/family/lineage IDs and all post-outcome fields. `gate_mismatch_fraction` is prohibited from blind diagnosis because the audited generator makes it nonzero for 90/90 synthetic program faults and zero for 180/180 other rows; it may appear only in a separately declared source-diff task where that source evidence is the intended input and that distinct target is adjudicated.

After selecting the finite frame and lineage roots, allocate entire roots to three locked partitions: development/training, probability calibration, and untouched test. The split manifest and seed are hashed before outcomes/labels are joined. Within development only, all preprocessing, feature/model selection and hyperparameter work use nested, lineage-grouped validation. Freeze the model, schema and thresholds before calibration; fit any probability calibrator only on the separate calibration families. Open the test once after all decisions are frozen. Related families and duplicate/near-duplicate cases cannot cross partitions; exact duplicate cases are collapsed or kept in one root, and near-duplicate cases require blinded lineage review before the split lock. No test feedback may be used to revise a model or protocol.

Partition proportions and family counts are deliberately **not assigned yet**: the independent frame, class prevalence, family-cluster size distribution and target error tolerances are unknown. Reviewers must set them from the target use and power/precision calculation before selection. The three roots in the technical pilot are not partition-size guidance.

## Endpoints, uncertainty and acceptance

Report (a) a true-row/predicted-column confusion matrix, per-class support, precision, recall/sensitivity, specificity and F1; macro-F1 and balanced accuracy; (b) classwise and overall multiclass Brier score and log loss; (c) calibration reliability plot, intercept/slope and a predeclared binned calibration error as descriptive only; (d) coverage, selective risk, classwise abstention and the cost-weighted abstention/error table; and (e) failed, unsupported, ambiguous, missing and excluded counts. State exact denominators. Give 95% intervals by resampling independent family roots (stratified only under a predeclared sampling design), with the number of roots and cluster-size distribution. Individual shots/runs are not independent units. Include sensitivity analyses for missing/ambiguous labels and any predeclared shift strata.

Acceptance has two layers. **Integrity gates** are deterministic: complete compatible source/config/runtime/seed/lineage provenance; no family or near-duplicate crossing; independent labels with blind adjudication; training-only transformations; calibration-only calibrator; complete required classes/support; one sealed-test analysis; reported classwise metrics and family-cluster intervals; and no protocol deviation. A failed integrity gate means no performance claim. **Use-specific statistical gates** must be set by the independent review panel before any test outcomes are opened: minimum useful classwise sensitivity/specificity and coverage, maximum classwise false-attribution and selective risk, calibration tolerance, confidence-bound decision rule, subgroup requirements, multiplicity control, and acceptable interval width. No defensible numerical floors are present in the repository, so none is invented here. Frozen existing diagnosis criteria remain separately failed and unchanged.

## Power and resource plan

The independent unit for power is the family root, not the row or shot. Before computing a numeric requirement, reviewers must supply: target class prevalence by family; expected evaluable/ambiguous/unsupported/nonresponse rates; cluster-size distribution; within-family intraclass correlation or defensible bounds; target sensitivity/specificity/calibration/coverage and their minimum acceptable confidence limits; alpha and familywise/multiplicity plan; desired power; and the planned estimator plus interval procedure. For a mean-like endpoint, a starting design-effect approximation is `DE = 1 + ((mean_cluster_size - 1) * ICC)` and `n_eff ≈ n_rows / DE`; unequal cluster sizes require a variance correction. This approximation is not adequate by itself for rare-class recall, abstention, correlated multi-class outcomes, or joint gates. The planned split and complete analysis must therefore be simulated at the family level under plausible low/base/high nuisance assumptions, with sensitivity analysis over unknown ICC, prevalence and cluster sizes. Choose the smallest family frame meeting all predeclared joint precision/power targets and the cost/resource cap; report failures if none fits. No current independent nuisance data or authorized target resource cap supports a numeric power claim. A separate reviewer must approve assumptions and reproducible analysis code. No expensive study is authorized by this protocol.
